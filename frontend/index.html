Vision Statement

To be a leading technical education institute producing skilled, ethical, and employable technicians capable of contributing effectively to engineering advancements and societal growth.

Mission Statement

  • M1: Provide quality diploma engineering education through practical-oriented teaching and well-equipped laboratories.
  • M2: Inculcate professional ethics, discipline, problem-solving skills, and environmental awareness among students.
  • M3: Strengthen institute-industry partnership to enhance practical exposure, internships, and placement opportunities.

Campus Facilities Infrastructure & Resources

Standard educational and technical infrastructure provided for diploma students at GP Awasari (Kh.):

Central Library

Equipped with curriculum textbooks, reference books, technical magazines, e-journals, and a quiet reading hall for students.

Computer Center & Labs

High-speed broadband internet facility, modern workstations, software programming compilers, and CAD/CAM software tools.

Engineering Laboratories

Departmental practical laboratories for Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer, Electronics, and Science experiments.

Classrooms & Audio-Visual

Spacious, well-ventilated lecture halls equipped with green boards, projection systems, and academic seating arrangements.

Sports & Gymnasium

Outdoor sports field for cricket, volleyball, kabaddi, and indoor games facilities to promote student physical fitness.

Workshop & Hostel

Central workshop for fitting, carpentry, welding, machining practicals, along with student residential hostel accommodation.
